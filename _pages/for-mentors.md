## Why join FAST? (Testimonials)

> Increasing accessibility of science, and helping people with fewer opportunities to engage with science

> Addressing fundamental injustices of accessing science and science education

> Fun and like seeing younger people liking to learn science

## Scheduling and Logistics

We meet approximately every other Saturday and carpool from Stanford to the appropriate school. Lunch and dinner is included.
