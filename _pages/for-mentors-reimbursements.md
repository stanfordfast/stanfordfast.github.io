## Purchasing


Do you need to purchase items for your students' project? Please see our inventory spreadsheet [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1KjuDQ0ghB4OzHO_AS51lIg26U8JUBBJTzvyViZZDtec/edit?usp=sharing) to look for what we have avaiable at each school. Otherwise you can place an purchase order [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1TYA5fkA1_0Z9sPK1w7QfPQ4e3MJMe1Pik4iirmcfy7k/edit). Each student has $100 budget for their project. Contact your school's CPOs with any questions.


## Reimbursement

Made any purchases for FAST? Please fill out the [reimbursement form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdrjE1X99xETdRD5zwXB48pWlswqhidSnpi77LLwmY4F_KpqA/viewform?usp=header). Note all the instructions at the top of the form!
